Pnyxy — AI-assisted reading and learning platform

Pnyxy is a web app for reading PDFs and EPUBs with AI assistance. Annotate as you read, ask an AI tutor questions about the text, build vocabulary flashcards with FSRS spaced repetition, take structured notes, and discuss readings with the community. Available on web, desktop, and mobile — free to use without an account on the public catalog.
